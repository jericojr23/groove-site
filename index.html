Lapat

Lapat helps you settle into focused practice. Start a timer, stop when it ends, and jot down one thing to try next time.

Lapat is Tagalog for well-fitted: a challenge that fits your skill, a target that fits your weak spot. Practise a little at a time, like a shell growing one close-fitting turn after another.

Your sessions stay on your iPhone. No account or tracking.
