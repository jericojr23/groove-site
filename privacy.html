Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Groove collects no data. There is no account, no server and no sign-in. Nothing you put into Groove is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

Your skills, sessions, ratings, reflections, Inbox notes and settings are saved only in Groove's storage on your iPhone. We can't see them, and there is no copy anywhere else.

Like other app data, this storage can be included in your iPhone's backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), depending on your iPhone's settings. Those backups are handled by Apple and your device, not by Groove.

What Groove doesn't do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • No network requests, except when you open a link (like this page) in the in-app browser.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Groove schedules them on your iPhone to tell you when a focus block or break ends. They are created and delivered on the device; no notification service or server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) when a page is opened. Groove itself never receives them.

Deleting your data

In Groove, go to Settings → Erase all data to remove everything Groove has saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it too.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
