Support

Have a question, run into a problem, or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub Issues.

I'm not getting a notification when a block ends

  • Check that notifications are on for Lapat in iOS Settings → Notifications → Lapat. Lapat's own Settings tab shows whether they are on.
  • A Focus mode may delay or silence notifications. Check your Focus settings.
  • With the app open on the Timer, Lapat plays a short chime instead of showing a banner. The silent switch turns the chime off.

Where is my data? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Everything is saved only on your iPhone; there is no account or cloud sync. Restoring a new iPhone from a backup of the old one (iCloud or your computer) brings Lapat's data with it.

How do I start over?

Go to Settings → Erase all data. This removes your skills, sessions, reflections, Inbox notes and settings, and can't be undone.

Why does one missed day not break my streak?

Research on habit formation found that missing a single day didn't materially affect it, so Lapat forgives one missed day. Miss two days in a row, and your streak ends.
